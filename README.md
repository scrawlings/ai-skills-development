# ai-skills-development

A record of various skills that I've been tinkering with.

- [disciplined-research](skills/disciplined-research/SKILL.md) two things came together, news stories of government reports and academic papers where naive use of AI produced bad results, and some research of my own getting variable results. I wanted a skill that guide results and guide the researcher to look deeper and work in a serious and professional way with citation focused research work.
- [fiction-framework](skills/fiction-framework) is a bunch of tools I've exploring to support writing fiction, particularly thinking about fantasy and science-fiction, with a process of note taking and idea exploration to support clarity and focus when I want to write. [world-bible](skills/fiction-framework/world-bible/SKILL.md) is the place to start as it sets up a schema of supporting documents and directories to keep everything in order. Working with this for a little while has me wanting to change lots of details, so I expect this will evolve or have superceeding versions in future.
